The product

Customers pay for name recommendations based on numerology rules relating a name to a date of birth. The product turns that service into an online journey with recommendations and payments.

What I owned

Built the website, recommendation engine and payment flow from scratch. Owned the product logic, launch, customer acquisition and ongoing improvements.

Used Claude and ChatGPT to generate and refine code, with Vercel, Supabase and GitHub supporting the application. Meta Suite, Zoho and Zixflow supported acquisition and customer operations.

Where AI fits

Built with AI. Recommendations run on rules.

The application does not need a model call for every recommendation. AI-assisted development helped turn the defined logic into a working product; the runtime recommendation engine follows numerology rules.

The business result

Generated ₹2.5 lakh in August 2026 revenue at a 60% profit margin. These are the figures in my latest resume, not a recurring-revenue or annualised projection.

Building independently made product scope, acquisition spending and commercial performance part of the same set of decisions.
