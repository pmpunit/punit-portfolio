The problem

An error spike tells a developer that something is wrong. It does not tell them where to begin. Large volumes of trace data can turn investigation into a search for a needle in a haystack.

The opportunity was to make tracing more useful in the moments developers need answers—and to extend coverage so fewer services remain invisible.

The strategy

Owned the long-term Distributed Tracing strategy, using Entity Coverage Ratio to measure the reach of instrumentation. The FY28 roadmap targets growth from 34% to 80%; this is a future goal, separate from achieved adoption results.

What I built

Intelligent Exemplar: where should I look?

Built the experience to help developers find representative traces without losing the context of their investigation. Owned prototyping, customer validation, evaluation and launch work.

Attribute Correlation Analysis: what is different?

Built ACA to compare anomalous traces with a healthy baseline and surface attributes associated with errors or slow responses. AI-generated explanations help developers interpret those signals.

Vibe-coded an ACA prototype to test ideas with customers, then owned prompt design, output evaluation and iterative refinement.

Product judgment

AI is most useful here when it explains evidence and helps a developer choose the next step.

ACA surfaces correlations, not guaranteed causes. The experience must keep the underlying evidence accessible so developers can verify a finding before acting.

The result

Investigation improvements, including Intelligent Exemplar and ACA, contributed to 16% growth in monthly active users between August 2024 and June 2026. This is a broader adoption outcome, not a standalone causal measurement for either feature.
