The spike is only the starting point

When latency or errors spike, the developer knows users are affected. The difficult questions remain: which requests should they inspect, what makes those requests different, and where should the investigation go next?

Millions of traces can hold the clues without making them easy to find. Searching for relevant evidence and interpreting it adds work while customers are still experiencing the problem.

A direction beyond features

I owned the long-term Distributed Tracing strategy around two connected needs: make more services visible, and make the information useful during an investigation. Entity Coverage Ratio became a measure of that reach, with a roadmap target of 34% to 80% by FY28.

First, where should I look?

Built features like Intelligent Exemplar to solve the needle-in-a-haystack problem: surface representative traces while preserving the context of the investigation.

Owned prototyping, customer validation, evaluation and launch work, making the proposed experience concrete enough to test with the people using it.

Then, what makes this different?

Attribute Correlation Analysis compares anomalous traces with a healthy baseline to identify attributes associated with errors or slow responses. AI-generated explanations help developers interpret those patterns and choose the next step.

I vibe-coded an ACA prototype, gathered customer feedback, and owned prompt design, output evaluation and iterative improvements. The work connected hands-on experimentation to a customer-facing investigation experience.

Keep the evidence visible. A correlation is a lead to investigate—not a guaranteed root cause.

What changed

Improvements across the investigation experience, including Intelligent Exemplar and ACA, contributed to 16% growth in monthly active users from August 2024 to June 2026.

The contribution was a more useful path through the data: a place to start, patterns to examine, and explanations grounded in evidence.
