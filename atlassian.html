The ambition

Owned end-to-end incident management in Jira Service Management and shaped a long-term strategy toward automated incident resolution: progress from playbooks and runbooks to intelligent resolution informed by past incidents.

That ambition depends on knowledge. If teams do not record how incidents were resolved, future workflows have little to learn from.

The missing step

Writing a post-incident report is valuable work, but it is still work after the immediate disruption has ended. Major-incident closure and PIR creation were at 0.2%.

Make capturing the lesson easier, so incident knowledge can become a foundation for future resolution.

What I built

Built automated post-incident report creation using generative AI and context from incident activity and related IT operations objects, such as alerts. Users receive a contextual draft to review and approve.

Owned the product end to end, alongside improvements across response, triage and the wider incident management journey.

Earning trust

Started with Atlassian as customer zero, then evaluated generated summaries against reports written by customers in shadow testing. Customer testing and preview releases provided further checks before broader launch.

Each phase had quality gates. The intended accuracy threshold was above 80%; this was a release criterion, not a claim of a measured final accuracy score.
