The data was there. The answer was buried.

When an application slowed down or failed, developers faced vast amounts of trace data without a clear starting point. Finding a useful trace was only half the challenge; understanding what made it different required further investigation.

MY RESPONSE

Built Intelligent Exemplar to surface relevant evidence and Attribute Correlation Analysis to identify patterns and explain them with AI.

Incidents were logged in JSM. Resolution happened elsewhere.

Customers saw JSM as a ticketing system. Responders, collaboration and stakeholder updates happened outside it, while users wanted incidents resolved with less manual effort.

MY RESPONSE
